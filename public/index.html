Pingback

One link to your resume that tells you how many times it was really opened.

How it works

  1. Your resume stays on Google Drive.
  2. You share one link, /cv, everywhere you apply.
  3. Every open is counted. Link previews from LinkedIn, WhatsApp and Slack are ignored, and so are your own clicks.

Try it

Open the demo link on any phone or laptop. It goes to a sample resume, and this counter goes up.
